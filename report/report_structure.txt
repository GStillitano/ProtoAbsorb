1. Abstract

* **The Problem:** Test-Time Adaptation (TTA) is crucial, but its success hinges entirely on accurately distinguishing between closed-set and open-set data.
* **Current State:** Existing methods rely heavily on high-confidence detection and hard thresholding; they fail to leverage the $L_1$ norm directly.
* **Our Approach (Cassano):** * Introduces a **soft, hyperplane alignment score** operating directly on the $L_1$ norm.
* Preserves a strong Out-of-Distribution (OOD) signal that is typically lost under covariate shift.
* Validated empirically on **CIFAR10-C**.
